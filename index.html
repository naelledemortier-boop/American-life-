Lesson 1 · Greetings
Say hello and introduce yourself
5–8 minutes · Vocabulary + speaking

In English, we use different greetings depending on the situation.

Hello! = Bonjour !
Hi! = Salut !
Good morning! = Bonjour (le matin).
Nice to meet you. = Enchanté(e).

Tip: “Hi” is friendly and very common in everyday American English. “Hello” works in both casual and more formal situations.
